# Code of conduct

Terset is a small project. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome, whatever their background or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, in issues, discussions and pull requests.
- Talk about the code and the idea, not the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and give the reason when you disagree.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Jokes or comments about a person's identity, body or background.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, or repeated posts after a maintainer asked you to stop.

## How to report

Use "Report content" on the comment, issue or pull request, and choose to report it to the repository maintainers. Reports stay private. For a security problem, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens next

A maintainer reads every report. Depending on what happened, we may edit or hide content, lock a thread, or block someone from the project. We tell the reporter what we did when we can.

This code applies in every space of this project on GitHub.
